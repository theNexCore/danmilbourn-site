HISTORIC SCOTT JOPLIN HOUSE

The State of Missouri acquired the property owned by the legendary ragtime musician, Scott Joplin.  The entire back wall of this historic building had fallen into ruin.  Dan Milbourn Construction was selected to rebuild the wall not only to meet historical specifications/standards, but to restore the beauty and functionality of this (now) historic site and museum.
