ABOUT US

Established in 1969

BUILT IN ST. LOUIS. BUILT TO LAST.

Dan Milbourn Construction, Inc. was founded in 1969 by third-generation mason Dan Milbourn. Nearly six decades later, the company remains family-owned and operated, serving homeowners, businesses, institutions, and historic properties throughout the St. Louis region.
Our reputation has been built the same way as our work: one project at a time. From masonry restoration and tuckpointing to brick, stone, chimney, porch, patio, and specialty repair work, our focus remains on quality craftsmanship, honest service, and doing the job right.
The Milbourn family leads the company day to day: Dan Milbourn (President), Dan Milbourn Jr. (Estimator), Chris Milbourn (On-Site Superintendent), and Tina Milbourn (Office Manager).

​OUR COMMITMENTS

Our commitment to safety and quality is reflected in every project we undertake. We hold A+ ratings from Angie's List and HomeAdvisor, along with A+ Better Business Bureau accreditation. We have developed an extensive safety plan, hold daily job site safety meetings with our crews, and every one of our employees is OSHA 10 certified.

Family owned masonry contractor serving homeowners and businesses across the St. Louis area since 1969.
