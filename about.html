Established 1969

Built on craftsmanship. Carried on by family.

Dan Milbourn Construction, Inc. was founded in 1969 by third-generation mason Dan Milbourn. Nearly six decades later, the company remains family-owned and operated, serving homeowners, businesses, institutions and historic properties throughout the St. Louis region.

Our story

One project at a time.

Our reputation has been built the same way as our work: one project at a time. From masonry restoration and tuckpointing to brick, stone, chimney, porch, patio and specialty repair work, our focus remains on quality craftsmanship, honest service, and doing the job right.

We are a small enough company that the family is on the job, and established enough that the National Park Service has trusted us with some of the most significant masonry in St. Louis.

History

A St. Louis company.

1969

Dan Milbourn Construction is founded

Established by Dan Milbourn, a third-generation mason, in St. Louis.

1985

A permanent home on Texas Avenue

The company moves into 2621 Texas Ave. in St. Louis — the same office it works from today.

2018

Dan Milbourn Sr. retires

Dan Milbourn Jr. is appointed President, carrying the company into its second generation of family leadership.

Today

Still family-owned and operated

The Milbourn family continues to run the company, serving homes, businesses, institutions and historic properties across the region.
