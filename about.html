ABOUT US

Established in 1969

​FOR OVER 45 YEARS, DAN MILBOURN CONSTRUCTION INC
​
HAS BEEN BEEN THE SOLUTION TO ST. LOUIS’ MASONRY NEEDS.

Founded in 1969 by Dan Milbourn, a third generation mason, we are a small family owned and ran business that is flexible and able to cater to your needs. We have the skills that have earned us a remarkable reputation as a leading masonry contracting company. Dan Milbourn (President), Dan Milbourn Jr (Estimator), Chris Milbourn (On Site Superintendent) and Tina Milbourn (Office Manager) has combined their strengths to create an unbeatable force in the masonry industry. We promised at the time we launched Dan Milbourn Construction Inc that we would always operate with a quality foundation of unwavering ethics and quality workmanship.

​OUR COMMITMENTS

Our commitments to Safety & Quality are industry leading. In addition to A+ ratings for quality from the best of the best including: Angie's List, Home Advisor and an A+ Better Business Bureau Accreditation, We have developed an extensive safety plan, hold daily job site safety meetings with their employees and each of their employees are OSHA 10 certified.
