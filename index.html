HISTORIC SCOTT JOPLIN HOUSE

The State of Missouri acquired the property owned by the legendary ragtime musician, Scott Joplin.  The entire back wall of this historic building had fallen into ruin.  We rebuilt the wall meeting historical specifications.  This historic site has been turned into a museum.
